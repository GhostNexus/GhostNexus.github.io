About

Meet Everleigh

Everleigh Brouse is a young actor with a genuine love for storytelling and a growing body of on-camera and stage work.

She approaches every role with focus and imagination, drawing on active training in scene study, improvisation, and on-camera technique. Directors describe her as prepared, coachable, and quick to find the truth of a moment.

Away from set, Everleigh brings the same energy to the things she loves — a curiosity and discipline that translate directly into her work as a performer.

She is represented for and available to audition for film, television, commercial, and theatre projects.
